## What this proposal is

A local SEO engagement for a business that is losing search traffic to
competitors in its own city. The reader is the owner — a tradesperson, a clinic
principal, a shop owner. They are not marketers and do not want to become one.

## What actually persuades this reader

Their own numbers, said plainly. "Six keywords, 2,600 searches a month between
them, you appear in the top ten for none" beats any amount of methodology. Use
the keyword table and the audit findings; they are the argument.

Name the competitor who is ranking instead of them if one was supplied. Owners
know exactly who that is and care more about it than about anything else in the
document.

## What loses them

Explaining what SEO is. Describing your process as though the process is the
product. Any sentence that would be equally true of a dentist and a plumber.

## Section notes

- `headline` — one sentence, their situation, their trade, their city.
- `intro` — the numbers, then what is causing them. No pitch yet.
- `technical_issues` — audit findings only. If the audit failed, return an empty
  array rather than guessing; the section simply will not render.
- `phases` — ground each phase in a specific issue found. "Rewrite the three
  duplicate service pages" not "on-page optimisation".
- `projection_note` — protect the company. Say plainly that the trajectory is a
  projection and that Google decides rankings.
