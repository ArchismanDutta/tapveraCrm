## What this proposal is

A Google Business Profile recovery — usually a suspension, sometimes a profile
that exists but never ranks. For an emergency trade a suspension is close to
total: they are simply absent from the map at the moment a customer needs them.

## Tone

Urgent because the situation is urgent, never alarmist. The owner already knows
something is wrong; they do not need to be frightened, they need to be told
precisely what happened and what it takes to undo it.

## What actually persuades this reader

The weekly cost, in their own currency, derived from their own enquiry numbers.
And specificity in the diagnosis: "your homepage says Gold Coast and your
contact page says Tweed Coast" is worth more than every generic claim combined,
because it proves someone actually looked.

## Honesty is a selling point here

Google, not the agency, decides reinstatements. Say so. A proposal that admits
the limit of what can be promised is more credible than one that does not, and
the guarantees list — which you do not write — already commits to it.

## Section notes

- `headline` — for a suspension, say they are GONE from Maps, not that they rank
  badly. The distinction is the entire point.
- `loss_bullets` — concrete removals: local pack position, hidden reviews,
  direction requests, click-to-call. Each one a thing they had last month.
- `suspension_causes` — observed evidence only. Quote the conflicting text, the
  missing address, the mismatched number. Never speculate about Google's
  reasoning; Google does not publish it.
- `existing_strengths` — real assets found in the audit. This section stops the
  document reading as an attack on their business.
