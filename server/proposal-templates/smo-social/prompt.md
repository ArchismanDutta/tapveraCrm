## What this proposal is

Ongoing social media management. The reader is usually posting occasionally and
inconsistently, and suspects they should be doing more without knowing what.

## What actually persuades this reader

Naming which platform matters for their specific business, and being willing to
say which one does not. Telling a client to drop LinkedIn is the most credible
thing in a social proposal, because every competing agency will offer to manage
all five.

Their own follower and engagement numbers against what the cadence would build.

## What loses them

Treating engagement as the goal. The owner cares about enquiries. Connect the
content to the enquiry wherever the facts allow it.

## Section notes

- `headline` — the gap between what they have and what the work would produce.
  Specific to their trade.
- `intro` — real numbers. Name the platform that matters most and say why the
  others matter less.
- `content_pillars` — specific to this business, not universal categories. For
  an interiors studio that is "Before and after", not "Educational content".
  The `share` values must total exactly 100.
- `projection_note` — the projection assumes the cadence is maintained. Say it
  plainly; do not soften it into a promise.
